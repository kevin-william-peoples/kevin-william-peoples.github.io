# About Me

I am a Master's student in Cybersecurity at New York University, with a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, San Diego. My interests span cybersecurity and software engineering, particularly frontend software development and application security.

Within cybersecurity, I am interested in Blue Team and Red Team security, network security, security operations, and penetration testing. I am particularly interested in identifying and assessing vulnerabilities, understanding how attacks occur, and exploring how security incidents can be detected, mitigated, and prevented.

My goal is to apply my computer science and cybersecurity background to develop reliable software and help protect systems, applications, and networks against evolving cybersecurity threats.
