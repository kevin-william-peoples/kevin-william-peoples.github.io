# About Me

I am a Master's student in Cybersecurity at New York University with a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, San Diego. My interests span cybersecurity and software engineering, with a particular interest in front-end software engineering.

Within cybersecurity, I am interested in Blue Team and Red Team security, network security, Security Operations, and penetration testing. I am particularly interested in identifying and assessing vulnerabilities, understanding how attacks occur, and learning how security incidents can be detected, mitigated, and prevented.

My goal is to apply my computer science and cybersecurity background to help build and protect secure systems, applications, and networks against evolving cybersecurity threats.
